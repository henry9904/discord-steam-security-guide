# 공식 출처 / Official sources

확인일 / Reviewed: 2026-10-26

일반 원리의 근거이며 특정 사건의 진단 근거는 아닙니다. 관리자 대응 절차는 운영 제안입니다.
These sources support general guidance, not a diagnosis of a specific incident. The moderation procedure is a suggestion.

- **Discord — [My Discord Account was Hacked or Compromised](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/24160905919511-My-Discord-Account-was-Hacked-or-Compromised)**
  - KO: 탈취 신고 · 인증된 앱 · 비밀번호
  - EN: Compromise reports, authorized apps, passwords
- **Discord — [Securing Your Discord Account](https://discord.com/safety/securing-your-discord-account)**
  - KO: QR 로그인 사기 · 비밀번호 변경 시 토큰 무효화
  - EN: QR login scams; password reset invalidates the current token
- **Discord — [My Discord Email was Changed and I Want to Undo It](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/27299669812375-My-Discord-Email-was-Changed-and-I-Want-to-Undo-It)**
  - KO: 48시간 복구 링크 · 복구 후 인증 재설정
  - EN: 48-hour recovery link; re-enrolling authentication after recovery

  - KO: 인증된 기기 · 전체 로그아웃 · Steam Guard
  - EN: Authorized Devices, Sign out everywhere, Steam Guard
