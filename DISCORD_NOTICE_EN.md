**Discord / Steam account takeover prevention & recovery**

We’ve put together a Korean/English guide explaining how takeovers happen, why they can happen again, and how to prevent and respond to them.

If a friend unexpectedly asks you to test a game, vote, claim free Nitro, approve a QR login, or run a file or command, verify through another contact method.

If you are affected, do not enter new passwords on a device you suspect is infected. Use a trusted device to **secure email → change account passwords and end sessions → review authorized apps and 2FA**. Check the device separately too.

🇰🇷 한국어: https://henry9904.github.io/discord-steam-security-guide/
🇺🇸 English: https://henry9904.github.io/discord-steam-security-guide/en.html

Do not forward suspicious messages; let a moderator know. Please do not blame or shame victims. Prompt reporting and response help limit further harm.
